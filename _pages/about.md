I study **graph machine learning**, with an interest in its theoretical foundations and practical applications. I am a Ph.D. student at RWTH Aachen University, supervised by [Prof. Dr. Christopher Morris](https://chrsmrrs.github.io/).

Previously, I earned my M.Sc. at the Technical University of Munich, advised by [Prof. Dr. Stephan Günnemann](https://www.professoren.tum.de/guennemann-stephan), and my B.Eng. at Zhejiang University, where my thesis was supervised by [Prof. Dr. Ronghao Zheng](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=LxgdmqYAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).
